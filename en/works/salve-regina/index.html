Salve Regina: Symphony of Mercy

A BANDA 6 LATINOS composition inspired by the Salve Regina prayer, written for choir and orchestra.

The piece moves from contemplation to symphonic intensity. Its choral and orchestral writing can support a reflective scene, a ritual or a revelation in film or television.

Composer: Benvindo Pereira da Silva Ferreira
